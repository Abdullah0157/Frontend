## Scoring Rubric

For each competency: pick a score from 1–9, cite evidence, note confidence.

**Score meanings** (from framework):
- **1** — Well below expectations for L4
- **3** — Below expectations
- **5** — Meets some expectations, gaps present
- **7** — Meets L4 expectations, would hire
- **9** — Exceeds — rare, reserve for exceptional
- **N/E** — Transcript did not surface enough evidence to score
